# Business Use Cases

The starter is a horizontal foundation that specializes into many verticals by
changing tokens, terminology and seed data — not architecture. The in-app
`/showcase` page presents these interactively for sales demos.

## Value proposition

- **For the vendor:** ship a branded, secure client portal in days; a strong
  client-acquisition and retention asset; a portfolio/reference architecture.
- **For the client:** one trustworthy place for status, support, documents,
  billing and communication — fewer emails, faster answers, more transparency.

## Vertical adaptations

| Vertical          | Rename modules to…                   | Adds                                     | Business value                               |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Legal firm**    | Matters, Case documents, Retainers   | Conflict checks, privileged watermarks   | Confidential, auditable client collaboration |
| **Healthcare**    | Care plans, Records, Appointments    | HIPAA consent, scheduling, PHI controls  | Patient engagement + controlled access       |
| **Logistics**     | Shipments, PODs, Exceptions          | Live tracking, SLA alerts                | Self-service visibility; fewer status calls  |
| **Financial**     | Holdings, Statements, Fees           | Performance charts, e-sign, step-up auth | Trust via transparency; compliant comms      |
| **Insurance**     | Policies, Claims, Premiums           | Claims workflow, adjuster assignment     | Faster claims intake; better retention       |
| **Consulting**    | Engagements, Deliverables, Retainers | Milestones, T&M tracking                 | Premium, organized client experience         |
| **Property mgmt** | Units, Maintenance, Rent             | Photo uploads, recurring rent            | Streamlined maintenance & collections        |
| **Education**     | Programs, Resources, Tuition         | Enrollment, gradebook, guardian access   | Centralized student/parent experience        |

## Cross-industry outcomes

- **Reduced support load** — self-service status and knowledge base.
- **Faster cash** — online invoice visibility and payment.
- **Stronger trust** — security posture, audit trail, professional UX.
- **Operational leverage** — one platform, many client relationships.

## Who it's for

Consulting firms, MSPs, SaaS, law firms, healthcare providers, logistics,
financial and insurance firms, real estate/property managers, educational
institutions, agencies, and B2B companies generally.
